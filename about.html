AW Technologies

AW Technologies operates under the umbrella of AW Traders and provides technical and engineering solutions.

Our primary technical focus is solar photovoltaic systems and related engineering services.

TECHNICAL FOCUS

Solar PV Solutions

Our solar-focused services cover different stages of a PV project, from assessment and design through installation and ongoing support.

PV System Design

Solar PV sizing, system design and technical planning.

Solar Consultancy

Technical assessment and feasibility support for solar projects.

Project Execution

Solar installation and project execution support.

System Optimization

Solutions and technical support aimed at improving PV system performance.

Zero-Export Solutions

PV solutions for applications requiring controlled export to the electrical grid.

Operation & Maintenance

Ongoing technical support and maintenance for solar PV systems.
